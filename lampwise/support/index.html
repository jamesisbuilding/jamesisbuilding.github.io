Support

Contact us

Email jamesrhyspotter@gmail.com. A person answers in 1 business day or less.

The quickest way is from the app: open Settings and tap Support. The email then includes the app version, the phone model and the system version, and you can attach the lock log if you want.

If your apps did not lock or did not unlock, use "Report a lock problem" in Settings or on the lock screen.

How to cancel a subscription

  1. In Lampwise, open Settings and tap "Cancel subscription". This opens your App Store subscriptions.
  2. Or, on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lampwise Pro, then Cancel Subscription.

To stop a free trial from charging, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. If you cancel, Pro stays to the end of the period you paid for. Then Lampwise stays free. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Questions

How does the lock work?

Choose the apps that pull you in, set a time, and set how many minutes you read first. At that time each day, those apps lock. Read, and they open as soon as your minutes are done.

The timer counts only time with the passage on screen. It stops if you leave the app or the screen turns off, and carries on when you come back.

Lampwise uses Screen Time to lock the apps you choose. It locks only those apps, and it never sees what you do in them.

What if I need my phone right now?

Use the emergency unlock. It is always there, on the Free tier too. Emergency unlocks never cost anything and have no limit. They do not count as a reading.

Can I use Lampwise without the lock?

Yes. You can read and keep your streak with no lock. Turn on the lock at any time in Settings.

What is free?

The Free tier has no time limit. It includes one daily lock, the reading timer and unlock, the emergency unlock, 6 translations, any book and chapter, highlights and saved verses, font size and dark mode, the streak, the verse of the day and the widget.

Pro adds more than one lock a day, re-lock, choosing which apps unlock, Reading Paths, 2 Grace Days a month and a Reflection Journal. Pro Monthly is $7.99 a month, Pro Yearly $49.99 a year and Family Yearly $79.99 a year, each with a 7-day free trial. Lifetime is $129.99, paid once. Prices shown in US dollars; your local App Store price may differ.

How does the free trial work?

Pro Monthly, Pro Yearly and Family Yearly start with 7 days free. The app shows the date your trial ends and the price. 2 days before the charge, you get a reminder. After the trial, the App Store charges your Apple ID unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

Pro is tied to your Apple ID, so there is no account to sign in to. On a new phone or after a reinstall, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open the plans in Lampwise and tap Restore. If no purchase is found, you keep the Free tier.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. You can start a refund request from inside Lampwise, or at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple answers by email.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, tap "Delete all data on this phone". This deletes your locks, lock log, answers, highlights, saved verses, notes, reading history and streak. It does not cancel your subscription.

To delete the analytics and purchase records held by our service providers, email jamesrhyspotter@gmail.com. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Lampwise never asks you to make an account or sign in.
