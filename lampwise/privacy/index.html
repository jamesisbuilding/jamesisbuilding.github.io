Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the Lampwise app ("Lampwise", "we") collects, why, who handles it for us, and how to have it deleted. The short version: Lampwise has no account, keeps your reading and your answers on your phone, and does no tracking.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is James Potter, the maker of Lampwise. Contact: jamesrhyspotter@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

These never leave your device. We cannot see them:

  • Your answers to the optional setup questions, including anything about your faith or your reading history.
  • Your notes, highlights, saved verses, reading history, streak and lock times.
  • The names of the apps you lock. iOS gives Lampwise only opaque tokens, not app names.

What we never collect

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA). Lampwise never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you.
  • Your location. IP geolocation is turned off in Mixpanel, so no city or country is stored from your IP address.
  • A name, a phone number or a password. There is no account.
  • Your contacts, photos, health data or browsing history.

Who handles data for us

Mixpanel, Inc. processes product analytics for us. Lampwise sends to Mixpanel's EU servers (api-eu.mixpanel.com), so the data stays in the EU. IP geolocation is off, no advertising ID is used, and nothing is used for tracking or ads.

RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchases for us, so that your subscription works and can be restored.

Both act only on our instructions. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use ad networks or data brokers.

Screen recordings

Screen recordings help us find and fix problems. They are off until you turn them on in Settings, under "Share screen recordings". When on, a recording is made in about 1 in 5 sessions and uploads only on Wi-Fi. Your reading, notes and messages stay hidden. Turn the switch off at any time and recording stops.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: in Settings, tap "Delete all data on this phone". This deletes your locks, lock log, answers, highlights, saved verses, notes, reading history and streak. It does not cancel a subscription. Deleting the app also removes this data.
  • With Mixpanel and RevenueCat: email jamesrhyspotter@gmail.com. We delete your Mixpanel profile and your RevenueCat customer record.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email jamesrhyspotter@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Data sent to Mixpanel and RevenueCat travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
