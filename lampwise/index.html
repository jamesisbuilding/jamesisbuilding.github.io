The morning scroll

  1. The alarm goes off.
  2. Your hand finds the phone.
  3. Social media is already open.
  4. Twenty minutes go by.

You meant to read first. Most mornings, the phone gets there before you do. Lampwise puts the Bible first in line.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose the apps that pull you in.

    Lampwise uses Screen Time to lock only the apps you pick, at the time you pick. It never sees what you do in them.

  2. 2

    Read today's passage.

    Set how many minutes you read first. Only time with the passage on screen counts. Put the phone down and the timer waits for you.

  3. 3

    Your apps open.

    As soon as your minutes are done, the lock lifts and you get on with your day.

Your reading stays on your phone.

  • Your answers, notes, highlights, saved verses, reading history and streak never leave your device.

Questions

How does the lock work?

Choose the apps that pull you in, set a time, and set how many minutes you read first. At that time each day, those apps lock. Read, and they open as soon as your minutes are done. Lampwise uses Screen Time, locks only the apps you choose, and never sees what you do in them.

What if I need my phone right now?

Use the emergency unlock. It is always there, on the Free tier too. Emergency unlocks never cost anything and have no limit. They do not count as a reading.

What is free?

The whole habit: one daily lock, the reading timer and the emergency unlock, with no time limit. See everything in Free.

What does Pro add, and what does it cost?

More locks a day, re-lock, Reading Paths, Grace Days and a Reflection Journal. Pro Monthly is $7.99 a month, Pro Yearly $49.99 a year and Family Yearly $79.99 a year, each with a 7-day free trial. Lifetime is $129.99, paid once. Prices in US dollars. See the plans.

Do I need an account?

No. Lampwise never asks you to make an account or sign in.
